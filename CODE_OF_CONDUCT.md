# Code of Conduct

Seamerge follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1, in issues, pull requests, discussions and every other place where the project meets its contributors.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work and not the person, and leave out harassment of any kind.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior is not acceptable, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or contact the maintainer, [cykedev](https://github.com/cykedev), on GitHub. Reports are handled privately, and the maintainer may remove content or block people who break this code.
